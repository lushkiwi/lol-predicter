Draft Predictor estimates the next pick or ban in a professional League of Legends draft, and each side's chance of winning, from past pro games.

The pick and ban probabilities are tested on held-out games: the actual choice is the model's first guess about 28% of the time and in its top five about 67%. The draft's effect on win probability is not validated and is shown as the model's opinion only.

Match data from Oracle's Elixir (non-commercial use). Champion data and images from Riot's Data Dragon. Typefaces: Sofia Sans, SIL Open Font License.
