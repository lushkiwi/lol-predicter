With 60 sampled negatives the same comparison was a tie (-0.0032, CI -0.0077 to +0.0013), so this is the second look at these features on the same validation games, and the gain is small.

## Decision

Serve the M3 regime trained on every candidate, as the mean logit of 3 seeds, with the pool-pinch features when the interval above excludes zero. The targeted-ban features stay out (worse on validation in `reports/player_targeting.md`). `experiments m3served` trains that model; the regime comparison and the ablations in `M3_models.md` keep the cheaper single-seed, sampled setting.
